# Pack Tools

## office_focus_note

Script: `tools/office_focus_note.tool.js`
Purpose: Turn the current office space snapshot into a short operational focus note.

## office_kanban_board

Script: `tools/office_kanban_board.tool.js`
Purpose: Draft a simple three-column kanban board body for `html_artifact_create` with kind `kanban_board`.

## office_standup_note

Script: `tools/office_standup_note.tool.js`
Purpose: Produce a concise standup-style note from the current office space snapshot.

## office_read_google_doc

Script: `tools/office_read_google_doc.tool.js`
Purpose: Read the plain text export of an accessible shared Google Doc from its URL.
